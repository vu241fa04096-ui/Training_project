🌱 Sowing & Harvest Window

Sowing: June - July (Kharif). Harvest: October - November.

🏞️ Soil Types Suitable

Clayey Loam, Alluvial, Silty Clay with strong moisture retention.

💧 Water & Irrigation Strategy

Flooded / continuous submergence 5-10 cm during active tillering.

🧪 Fertilizer Management

Split application of Nitrogen (Basal, Tillering, Panicle initiation). Requires adequate P & K for stem strength.

🛡️ Common Pests & Diseases

Stem borer, Brown planthopper, Blast, Bacterial leaf blight.

💰 Economic Market Value

Staple food crop feeding over 50% of world population. High domestic & export market demand.
